Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about your stay

What is the check-in / check-out time? +
Check-in is at 11:00 AM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available on request.
Is the villa suitable for families? +
Yes! The villa is perfect for families. It has a spacious living room, a garden for kids to play, and is just a short walk from the beach.
Is parking available? +
Yes, free private parking is available on the premises for all guests.
Are pets allowed? +
Unfortunately, pets are not allowed at the villa.
How far is the beach from the villa? +
Nandgaon Beach is just 90 metres away, about a 2-minute walk from the villa.
Is WiFi available? +
Yes, complimentary high-speed WiFi is available throughout the property.
