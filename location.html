Sightseeing & Attractions

Explore the best of what the Konkan coast has to offer

🌊

Nandgaon Beach

📍 90 metres (2 min walk)

A beautiful, less-crowded beach perfect for morning walks, swimming, and relaxing by the Arabian Sea.

🌊

Kashid Beach

📍 6.8 km (11 min drive)

One of the most popular beaches on the Konkan coast, known for its white sand, cliffs, and scenic beauty.

⛪

Murud-Janjira Fort

📍 14 km (36 min drive)

A historic sea fort dating back to the 15th century. One of the most impressive marine fortresses in India.

🛒

Siddhivinayak Temple

📍 500 metres

A serene temple located nearby. Seek blessings before your beach day.

🍴

Local Restaurants

📍 500 metres - 1 km

Enjoy fresh seafood and authentic Malvani cuisine at local restaurants and shacks near the beach.

🌏

Murud Beach

📍 21 min drive

A serene black-sand beach with food shacks, a relaxed vibe, and boat access to the historic fort.

🏛

Korlai Fort & Beach

📍 29 min drive

A historic Portuguese fort with a lighthouse offering panoramic views, located by a peaceful beach.

🎭

Revdanda Fort

📍 30 min drive

An abandoned fort surrounded by lush jungle and black sand beach. Great for exploration and photography.

🌎

Phansad Wildlife Sanctuary

📍 45 min drive

A protected area with diverse flora and fauna. Ideal for nature walks, bird watching, and trekking.

How to Reach

Getting to Parijat Villa is easy from major cities

🚆 From Mumbai

Drive via Mumbai-Goa Highway (NH-66) towards Alibaug. Take the route via Pen, then continue towards Murud Janjira. The drive takes approximately 3-4 hours.

🚆 From Pune

Take the Mumbai-Pune Expressway towards Panvel. Continue on NH-66 towards Alibaug and follow signs to Murud Janjira. Approximate drive time is 3.5-4.5 hours.

🚆 By Train

The nearest railway station is Roha (40 km away). You can take a taxi or local bus from Roha to Nandgaon. Alternatively, you can take a train to Mumbai and drive down.
