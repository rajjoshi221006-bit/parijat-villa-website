A Home Away From Home

Parijat Villa is a charming homestay located in the peaceful village of Nandgaon, along the beautiful Konkan coast of Maharashtra. Whether you are looking for a quiet weekend retreat or a family beach holiday, our villa offers the perfect escape.

Situated within 90 metres of Nandgaon Beach, the property features a spacious villa with a private terrace, a fully equipped kitchen, and a lush garden. We provide free private parking and WiFi for all guests.
