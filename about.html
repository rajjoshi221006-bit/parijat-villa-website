About Parijat Villa

Discover the story behind your perfect beachside homestay

Our Story

Nestled in the serene village of Nandgaon along the Konkan coast, Parijat Villa began as a personal dream to create a peaceful retreat where guests could experience the true essence of beachside living. What started as a family home soon became a beloved homestay, welcoming travellers from all over.

Our villa is designed to offer you the perfect blend of comfort and nature. With the beach just 90 metres away, you can wake up to the gentle sound of waves, enjoy a cup of tea on the terrace overlooking the garden, and take a short walk to the golden sands of Nandgaon Beach.

We take pride in offering a warm, personalised experience. Whether you are here for a quiet weekend escape, a family vacation, or a remote work retreat, Parijat Villa is your home away from home.
